Vertical Blinds

Manufactured & Fitted in Hull • Domestic, Commercial & Trade

Vertical Blinds are the most versatile and stylish blind suitable for any environment. Ideal for Bay Windows, Patio doors and Conservatories. The blinds can be drawn back for maximum light or angled to protect furniture and privacy. We offer a wide range of fabrics in contemporary or functional styles, also available are blackouts and ridged louvers.

Key Features & Options

  • 89mm & 127mm slat widths
  • Straight or curved headrail tracks
  • Ideal for bays, conservatories & patios
  • Change just the slats when you redecorate
  • Blackout, textured & wipe-clean fabrics
  • FREE precision measuring & fitting included
